Privacy Policy

Writune · Last updated: May 2026

Writune ("we", "our", or "the app") is an English transcription learning app for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Writune does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app does not require an account or login.

All user data (learning progress, settings, completed content) is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and is never sent to any external server.

2. Third-Party Content Sources

Writune fetches publicly available content from the following third-party sources to provide daily learning material. These requests do not include any personal data.

  • Wikipedia Featured Feed — en.wikipedia.org
  • The Guardian Open Platform — open-platform.theguardian.com
  • NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) — api.nasa.gov
  • BBC News RSS Feed — feeds.bbci.co.uk

Please refer to each provider's own privacy policy for details on how they handle data on their end.

3. Analytics & Tracking

Writune does not use any analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking libraries. We do not track your usage behavior.

4. Children's Privacy

Writune is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's built-in security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We have no access to your device or its data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
